# Privacy Policy for Clixen

**Last Updated: October 29, 2025**

**Effective Date: October 29, 2025**

## Introduction

Clixen ("we," "our," or "us") operates https://clixen.app (the "Service"), an AI-powered voice assistant for calendar management. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

By using Clixen, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: When you sign up, we collect your email address, name, and authentication credentials via Google OAuth.
- **Voice Data**: Audio recordings of your voice commands, which are temporarily processed to provide the Service and then deleted.
- **Calendar Data**: When you grant permission, we access your Google Calendar data to read, create, modify, and delete calendar events on your behalf.

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data**: We collect information about how you interact with the Service, including commands issued, features used, and response times.
- **Technical Data**: Device information, browser type, IP address, and connection metadata for security and performance monitoring.
- **Conversation History**: Your chat interactions with the AI assistant, stored securely to maintain conversation context and improve your experience.

### 3. Information from Third-Party Services

- **Google Account Data**: When you authenticate via Google, we receive your profile information (name, email, profile picture) and access to your Google Calendar as authorized by you.
- **Google AI Services**: We use Google's Gemini AI and Vertex AI to process your requests. Your queries are sent to Google's servers for processing.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Provide the Service**: Process voice commands, manage your calendar, and respond to your queries using AI.
2. **Improve the Service**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features, fix bugs, and optimize performance.
3. **Authenticate Users**: Verify your identity and maintain secure access to your account.
4. **Communicate**: Send important notices about service changes, security updates, or account issues.
5. **Ensure Security**: Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, and other harmful activity.
6. **Comply with Legal Obligations**: Respond to legal requests and prevent harm.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Clixen's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

### Specific Google Calendar API Usage

We request the following Google Calendar scopes:

- **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events`**: To read, create, update, and delete calendar events on your behalf based on your voice commands.
- **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar` (Full Access)**: To access calendar metadata and settings for advanced features like finding free time slots across multiple calendars.

**How We Use Calendar Data:**
- Read your calendar events to answer queries like "What's on my schedule today?"
- Create new events when you say "Schedule a meeting with John tomorrow at 3pm"
- Modify existing events when you request changes
- Delete events when you ask to cancel appointments
- Check availability across your calendars to find free time slots
- Detect scheduling conflicts before creating events

**We Do NOT:**
- Share your calendar data with third parties for marketing or advertising
- Use your calendar data to train AI models (data is processed in real-time only)
- Store calendar event content permanently (cached temporarily for conversation context, then deleted)
- Access calendars you haven't explicitly authorized

### Data Minimization

We only request and use the minimum necessary Calendar scopes to provide the core functionality. We do not request access to:
- Your Gmail
- Your Google Drive
- Your Google Photos
- Any other Google services not essential to calendar management

## Data Retention

- **Calendar Data**: Cached temporarily (up to 24 hours) for conversation context, then automatically deleted. We do not permanently store calendar events.
- **Voice Recordings**: Processed immediately and deleted within 24 hours. Not stored long-term.
- **Conversation History**: Retained for up to 90 days to maintain conversation context. You can delete this data at any time.
- **Account Information**: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
- **Authentication Tokens**: Stored securely until you revoke access or delete your account.
